# YT-StrategizeR

A local tool for studying YouTube titles and topics to decide what videos to make.

## Language

**Channel**:
A YouTube channel the user has imported and studies as a competitor or niche reference.

**Video**:
A single upload belonging to a Channel, with its title, publish date and public stats.

**Import**:
Adding a Channel by URL or handle, pulling its full Video list with metadata and stats.

**Sync**:
Re-pulling an imported Channel to pick up new Videos and refresh stats.

**Topic**:
A named group of Videos with similar subject matter, derived from Video titles. Every Video belongs to exactly one Topic. Users can rename and merge Topics.
